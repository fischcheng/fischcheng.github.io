This lunchtime webinar from the MIT Office of Sustainability and MIT Libraries features Yu Cheng (Sustainability Data Scientist), Alejandro Paz (Librarian for Energy and Environment), and Erin Shives (GIS & Statistics Librarian), who share how to access and work with sustainability data at multiple scales, from the MIT campus to the city, regional and global contexts.
